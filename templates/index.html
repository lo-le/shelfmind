How "the swarm chooses" works

Each shopper picks where to buy this product (Tesco, a competitor that stocks it, or a Tesco substitute) or walks away, by maximising utility U = price + deal + store loyalty + product appeal + social proof plus noise (a logit model). We run it twice with the same shoppers and noise - Tesco at base price, then with your promo - so the difference is the promo's causal effect, and we can trace each switcher's origin.
